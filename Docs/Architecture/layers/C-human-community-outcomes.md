# Layer C — Human and Community Outcomes

## User-supplied architectural purpose

Evaluates whether public services and investments produce meaningful, measurable improvements in people’s lives. Its scope includes education, healthcare, housing, food security, employment, public safety, mobility, environmental quality, civic participation, and integrated community well-being.

## Current repository scope

Dedicated synthetic aggregate outcome workspace: immutable measurement/cohort/exclusion/uncertainty contracts, revision-pinned baseline/follow-up observations, counter-measures, separate checks and descriptive deltas with missing/stale-data holds. No verified real outcome dataset, distributional inference or causal-effect assessment is delivered.
