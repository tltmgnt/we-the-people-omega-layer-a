# A4 — Courts and Justice

## Scope

Potential effects on adjudication, court access and review or remedy mechanisms.

These are proposed review prompts, not findings of applicable law. Apply the
[shared recording instructions](README.md) and document jurisdiction-specific sources and uncertainty.

## Review questions

- Does the proposal affect a court, administrative adjudication, enforcement process or access to review? Identify the forum and case type.
- Which actor can make the decision, and what authority determines jurisdiction, procedure and available remedies?
- What notice, opportunity to be heard, representation, impartiality and record-access questions arise?
- Could automation obscure reasons, evidence, errors or a route to human adjudication? What safeguards require verification?

## Evidence to request

- Forum, actor and jurisdiction map with applicable dated procedural sources.
- Process diagram covering notice, response, decision, reasons and review.
- Proposed records, access measures, conflict procedures and correction paths; use synthetic examples.

## Escalation triggers

- Uncertain forum, decision authority, procedural protections or remedy.
- Unresolved interference with impartial review or access to the decision record.

Under the proposed project workflow, unresolved triggers remain blockers and move the
packet to LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED for qualified, authorized human legal review. A checked
box or an evidence reference does not establish legal sufficiency.
