# A6 — Federalism

## Scope

Authority and responsibility across federal, state and local institutions, with a separate Tribal-sovereignty review.

These are proposed review prompts, not findings of applicable law. Apply the
[shared recording instructions](README.md) and document jurisdiction-specific sources and uncertainty.

## Review questions

- Which federal, state and local actors would act, fund, regulate, hold data or provide remedies?
- What authority and limits apply to each actor, including delegation, agreements and funding conditions?
- Are overlapping or conflicting requirements, preemption questions or intergovernmental disputes unresolved?
- Who remains accountable for notices, corrections, records and remedies across organizational boundaries? Screen separately for A7.

## Evidence to request

- Jurisdiction and responsibility matrix for each proposed action.
- Dated authorities, agreements, funding conditions and identified conflicts or competing interpretations.
- Cross-institution escalation, data-sharing, correction and remedy arrangements.

## Escalation triggers

- Unclear authority allocation, unresolved conflicting requirements or disputed jurisdiction.
- An agreement is treated as sufficient without assessment of each actor’s authority.
- Tribal implications are folded into state/local assumptions without separate A7 assessment.

Under the proposed project workflow, unresolved triggers remain blockers and move the
packet to LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED for qualified, authorized human legal review. A checked
box or an evidence reference does not establish legal sufficiency.
