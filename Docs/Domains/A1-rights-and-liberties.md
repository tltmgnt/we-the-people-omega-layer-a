# A1 — Rights and Liberties

## Scope

Potential effects on people’s rights, protected interests and practical access to public processes.

These are proposed review prompts, not findings of applicable law. Apply the
[shared recording instructions](README.md) and document jurisdiction-specific sources and uncertainty.

## Review questions

- Who may lose access, receive different treatment or face a burden under the proposal? Include indirect and digital-access effects.
- Which rights or protected interests may be implicated, and what jurisdiction-specific authority determines applicability?
- What notice, explanation, opportunity to respond, review or remedy needs assessment?
- What data is collected, inferred, shared or retained, and what authority and safeguards need verification?

## Evidence to request

- Affected-population and process maps, including accessible and nondigital routes.
- Dated applicable authorities, alternative interpretations and a rights-impact explanation.
- Proposed notices, appeal routes, data-flow and retention descriptions; evidence of their verification.

## Escalation triggers

- Unresolved rights applicability, exclusion or unequal treatment.
- Unverified notice, remedy, access or data-use safeguards.

Under the proposed project workflow, unresolved triggers remain blockers and move the
packet to LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED for qualified, authorized human legal review. A checked
box or an evidence reference does not establish legal sufficiency.
