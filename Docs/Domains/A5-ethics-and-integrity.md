# A5 — Ethics and Integrity

## Scope

Conflicts, influence, stewardship and accountability in the proposed institutional process.

These are proposed review prompts, not findings of applicable law. Apply the
[shared recording instructions](README.md) and document jurisdiction-specific sources and uncertainty.

## Review questions

- Who benefits, decides, supplies data or technology, reviews and audits? What relationships or incentives need disclosure?
- Which ethics, procurement, funding, disclosure and recordkeeping rules apply to these specific actors?
- How are conflicts identified, reviewed and addressed, including recusal or reassignment where applicable?
- Can concerns be reported and independently investigated, and what confidentiality and retaliation questions need review?

## Evidence to request

- Role and relationship map; appropriately handled conflict and interest disclosures.
- Dated applicable ethics and procurement sources; proposed conflict-management decisions.
- Audit responsibility, decision logs and reporting/investigation process descriptions.

## Escalation triggers

- Unresolved conflict, undisclosed influence or uncertain spending/procurement authority.
- No accountable reviewer for a concern, or unresolved reporting and retaliation risks.

Under the proposed project workflow, unresolved triggers remain blockers and move the
packet to LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED for qualified, authorized human legal review. A checked
box or an evidence reference does not establish legal sufficiency.
