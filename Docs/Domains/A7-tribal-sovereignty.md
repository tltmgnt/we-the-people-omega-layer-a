# A7 — Tribal Sovereignty

## Scope

Distinct Tribal authority, affected Tribal Nations and government-to-government relationships; assess separately from state/local government.

These are proposed review prompts, not findings of applicable law. Apply the
[shared recording instructions](README.md) and document jurisdiction-specific sources and uncertainty.

## Review questions

- Which Tribal Nations, lands, people, institutions, resources or data may be affected, and what facts determine jurisdiction?
- Which treaties, Tribal laws, federal sources, agreements and jurisdiction-specific decisions require examination by qualified reviewers?
- Which Tribal authorities and designated representatives should determine the appropriate government-to-government process?
- What consultation, consent, notice, data-governance or agreement requirements apply in this specific context, and what evidence of completion is needed?

## Evidence to request

- Affected-Nation and jurisdiction map that identifies uncertainty without treating missing information as absence of Tribal interests.
- Applicable dated sources and Tribal-authority input, with restricted materials handled through authorized channels.
- Proposed engagement and decision responsibilities; authorized records of any required process and unresolved objections.

## Escalation triggers

- Unknown affected Nations, disputed jurisdiction or unverified authority to represent a Tribal government.
- Required consultation or consent remains unresolved or is assumed satisfied by general public outreach.
- Unresolved Tribal data, resource, rights or government-to-government issues.

Under the proposed project workflow, unresolved triggers remain blockers and move the
packet to LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED for qualified, authorized human legal review. A checked
box or an evidence reference does not establish legal sufficiency.
