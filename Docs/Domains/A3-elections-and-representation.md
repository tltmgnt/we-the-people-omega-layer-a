# A3 — Elections and Representation

## Scope

Potential effects on election administration, participation and representative processes.

These are proposed review prompts, not findings of applicable law. Apply the
[shared recording instructions](README.md) and document jurisdiction-specific sources and uncertainty.

## Review questions

- Does the proposal affect registration, eligibility processing, voter information, ballots, counting, certification, boundaries or representation? Identify the exact function.
- Which election and jurisdiction are involved, and which authorized officials and current rules govern the proposed change?
- What access, language, disability, privacy, equal-treatment and Tribal-context questions require review?
- How are records, changes, errors and challenges handled, and which election-specific deadlines or preservation rules need verification?

## Evidence to request

- Function and jurisdiction map, accountable election officials and applicable dated sources.
- Change plan, access assessment and test results for the precise election function.
- Record provenance, incident handling, correction and challenge procedures.

## Escalation triggers

- Unknown election authority, applicable rules or deadline consequences.
- Unresolved participation barriers, unexplained differential treatment or unverified record integrity.

Under the proposed project workflow, unresolved triggers remain blockers and move the
packet to LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED for qualified, authorized human legal review. A checked
box or an evidence reference does not establish legal sufficiency.
