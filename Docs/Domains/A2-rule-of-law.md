# A2 — Rule of Law

## Scope

The authority, reasons, consistency and challengeability of a proposed institutional action.

These are proposed review prompts, not findings of applicable law. Apply the
[shared recording instructions](README.md) and document jurisdiction-specific sources and uncertainty.

## Review questions

- Who would act, and which current authority, delegation and limits support each material action?
- Are binding requirements distinguished from policy preferences and proposed project standards?
- What standards guide decisions, exceptions and explanations, and how are inconsistent outcomes examined?
- Which actor can review, correct, suspend or revoke a decision, and how can an affected person challenge it?

## Evidence to request

- Actor-to-authority map with dates, source references and delegation boundaries.
- Decision criteria, exception procedures and records of reasons.
- Challenge, correction and audit procedures with responsible owners.

## Escalation triggers

- Missing, disputed or expired authority or delegation.
- Unexplained exceptions, conflicting requirements or no identified correction mechanism.

Under the proposed project workflow, unresolved triggers remain blockers and move the
packet to LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED for qualified, authorized human legal review. A checked
box or an evidence reference does not establish legal sufficiency.
