# Government collaboration — concept and bounded-pilot brief

## Proposed initiative

WE THE PEOPLE Ω is a proposed nonpartisan civic-systems research, education, evaluation and
institutional-improvement initiative connecting five layers and a 50-project portfolio.
Its purpose is to identify systemic gaps, test improvements, measure human and operational
outcomes, and retain verified lessons. It does not claim government endorsement, validated
universal applicability, a selected agency or an authorized partnership.

## Requested initial step

Seek guidance on the appropriate office for framework review and a preliminary discussion
or bounded demonstration. Ask how to submit a concept paper, which existing evaluation or
modernization programs may align, and what research, data, procurement or partnership
requirements apply. No message is sent and no recipient or institutional fit is presumed.

## Proposed collaboration phases

| Phase | Deliverable | Gate before proceeding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Framework review | Assumptions, layer map, project register and limitations | Participating office agrees scope and responsible roles |
| Pilot selection | One low-risk process with purpose, owner and accessible baseline | Applicable authority, rights, jurisdiction and data access assessed |
| Baseline | Time/burden, resources, quality, access, outcomes and uncertainty | Reproducible definitions, usable data and independent review plan |
| Controlled intervention | Bounded practical change or simulation, comparison and safeguards | Separate authorized human decision, resources, continuity and rollback |
| Independent evaluation | Results, uncertainty, alternatives and unintended effects | Evidence supports adoption, further testing, revision or stopping |
| Reporting/learning | Lawful findings, limitations, corrections and durable ownership | Disclosure/retention rules and adoption responsibilities agreed |

## Candidate charter — permit process

Candidate Project 009 can use the synthetic permit exercise as a design aid. A records
request, benefits burden or maintenance process may be preferable if selected by the
participating institution. Do not assume the permit example is already selected or approved.

- Public purpose: reduce avoidable delay and burden while preserving accurate, accessible,
  safe and lawful service.
- Institution, jurisdiction and process boundary: to be selected and verified.
- Owners: operational, assurance, accountable decision maker and public-accountability
  roles to be assigned; independent evaluation responsibility documented.
- Data: only authorized proportionate records, with privacy, confidentiality, retention,
  security and disclosure requirements established first.
- Baseline: equivalent case definitions, elapsed/public/staff effort, rework, quality,
  appeals/access and continuity measures, with uncertainty and exclusions.
- Success criteria: agreed before intervention, including outcome/counter-metric
  thresholds, minimum usable evidence and a feasible comparison method.
- Causal evaluation: document alternatives and confounding; use the strongest feasible
  proportionate design and avoid attributing a before/after change automatically to the pilot.
- Stop/review conditions: unanticipated rights/privacy/safety harms, loss of continuity,
  unreliable data or exceeded agreed resource/scope limits.
- Rollback: owner, recoverable process/data versions and communication plan agreed
  before any live change; no removal of required legal/safety checks.
- Adoption: evidence-based human decision, sustained owner/funding/training, follow-up
  on whether results persist after temporary support ends.

## Analytical methods and benchmarking

TGA Ω, DTI Ω and HedgeOps Ω are proposed research methods. Test measurement properties
and decision usefulness. Benchmark multi-agent analysis against a documented checklist,
single-analysis baseline or simpler statistical approach using predefined quality, cost,
time, reproducibility and error criteria. Model agreement is not independent verification.

## Minimum discussion packet

Provide a concise concept summary, five-layer map, versioned project register with
unresolved entries, proposed charter, measurement/evaluation plan, safeguards, source
register and relative 90-day roadmap. Do not present the whole portfolio as an immediate
procurement request or require a centralized government database.

The provided leadership letter remains an unsent goal statement. Any actual outreach,
partner agreement, purchase, data connection or intervention needs its own explicit scope
and applicable institutional process.
