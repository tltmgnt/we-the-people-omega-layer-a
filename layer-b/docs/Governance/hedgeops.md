# HedgeOps Ω — proposed operational intelligence and assurance

These are analytical roles and future design requirements, not deployed agents.
This package only runs local validation; it has no external accounts or execution channels.

| Role | Proposed analysis |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | Reconcile budgets, commitments, payments and assets; flag unmatched items |
| Procurement | Check documentation, milestones, amendments and contract controls |
| Workforce/capacity | Examine workload, skills, training and continuity constraints |
| Process/temporal | Map queues, handoffs, delays, rework and transferred burden |
| Records/evidence | Inspect provenance, versions, missing information and traceability |
| Infrastructure/resilience | Examine asset condition, maintenance dependencies and scenarios |
| Legal/rights review support | Identify authority questions for qualified, authorized human legal review |
| Arbitration/assurance support | Compare findings and counter-hypotheses in a traceable brief |

## Required controls before any deployment

- Read-only and least privilege by default, limited to authorized data.
- Separate analysis, recommendation, approval and execution.
- Independent evidence checks with source traceability and reproducibility.
- Record disagreement, uncertainty and competing explanations.
- Require authorized human approval for consequential operational changes.
- No autonomous legal findings, employee discipline, vendor disqualification or denial
  of essential public services.
- Audit logs, correction channels, security assessment, rollback and continuity procedures.
- Keep protected records and credentials out of repository examples.

Agent agreement is not independent verification when models share data, assumptions
or underlying systems. An assurance role must document what evidence was independently
checked and how; a second model response alone does not establish independence.
