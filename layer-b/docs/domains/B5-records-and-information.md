# B5 — Records and information

## Objective

Maintain accurate records of institutional actions that support accountable operations and appropriate access, security and preservation.

These are proposed operational review prompts. Establish applicability with Layer A;
do not infer legal authority or actual institutional performance from checklist completion.

## Research modules

- Record creation and provenance
- Classification and retention schedules
- Version control and preservation
- Searchability and information quality
- Public-records requests and lawful exemptions
- Privacy, confidentiality and security
- Interagency data exchange
- Audit trails and records correction
- Digital preservation and continuity

## Review questions

- Can the institution reconstruct who acted, with which authority, evidence and version?
- Which retention, disclosure, privacy and access rules apply to these exact records?
- Can authorized users recover records without exposing protected information?

## Candidate measures

- Record completeness and retrieval success
- Time to locate records
- Request processing time by complexity and applicable law
- Duplicate, missing or inconsistent records
- Retention-schedule compliance
- Access-denial and appeal outcomes
- Preservation and recovery readiness
- Provenance and audit-trail completeness

Use the [indicator specification](../Methods/scorecard.md): definitions, denominators,
periods, exclusions, uncertainty, owner and revision. Pair speed/cost with quality,
access, safety and rework measures.

## Evidence and escalation

Request relevant authorized process maps, dated authority references, financial or service
records, verification results and responsible-owner attestations. Record missing information,
competing explanations and data restrictions. Unresolved authority or rights issues return
to Layer A review; operational uncertainty remains visible in the Layer B record.

## Interpretation safeguard

Manage transparency and privacy together. Public accountability does not justify unrestricted disclosure of protected information.
