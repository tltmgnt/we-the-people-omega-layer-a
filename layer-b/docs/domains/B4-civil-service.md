# B4 — Civil service and public workforce

## Objective

Assess whether institutions have the staffing, skills, leadership and working conditions needed to deliver services consistently.

These are proposed operational review prompts. Establish applicability with Layer A;
do not infer legal authority or actual institutional performance from checklist completion.

## Research modules

- Recruitment, hiring and onboarding
- Staffing levels and workload distribution
- Skills, training and professional development
- Retention, turnover and vacancy duration
- Institutional knowledge and succession
- Employee rights, labor relations and accommodations
- Workforce planning and budget alignment
- Burnout risk, operational resilience and service quality

## Review questions

- What demand, case complexity, tools and qualified capacity explain workload?
- Which skills and roles are single points of failure?
- What employee protections and human-review requirements apply to any proposed personnel action?

## Candidate measures

- Vacancy rate and duration
- Time to fill critical roles
- Workload relative to qualified capacity
- Training completion and demonstrated competency
- Retention and turnover by role
- Overtime, absence and workload distribution
- Service errors, rework and continuity
- Succession coverage for critical functions

Use the [indicator specification](../Methods/scorecard.md): definitions, denominators,
periods, exclusions, uncertainty, owner and revision. Pair speed/cost with quality,
access, safety and rework measures.

## Evidence and escalation

Request relevant authorized process maps, dated authority references, financial or service
records, verification results and responsible-owner attestations. Record missing information,
competing explanations and data restrictions. Unresolved authority or rights issues return
to Layer A review; operational uncertainty remains visible in the Layer B record.

## Interpretation safeguard

Do not attribute low productivity or backlogs to employees without examining system constraints. No autonomous discipline, promotion or employment findings.
