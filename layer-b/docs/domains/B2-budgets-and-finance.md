# B2 — Budgets and public finance

## Objective

Trace public resources from authorization to actual use and verified public outcomes.

These are proposed operational review prompts. Establish applicability with Layer A;
do not infer legal authority or actual institutional performance from checklist completion.

## Research modules

- Revenue and funding-source analysis
- Budget proposals and appropriations
- Allocation and obligation management
- Cash flow and expenditure
- Grants and intergovernmental transfers
- Capital budgeting and lifecycle costs
- Financial controls, reconciliation and audit
- Outcome and public-value evaluation

## Review questions

- Can funding be traced from authority and allocation through commitments, payments, acceptance and assets?
- Which accounting basis, reporting period and jurisdiction apply?
- Could underspending reflect delayed services, or a variance reflect demand rather than misconduct?

## Candidate measures

- Budget-to-actual variance
- Obligation and payment timing
- Cost per comparable completed service
- Unreconciled transactions
- Audit finding age and verified closure
- Capital project cost and schedule variance
- Lifecycle maintenance liabilities
- Outcomes relative to authorized objectives

Use the [indicator specification](../Methods/scorecard.md): definitions, denominators,
periods, exclusions, uncertainty, owner and revision. Pair speed/cost with quality,
access, safety and rework measures.

## Evidence and escalation

Request relevant authorized process maps, dated authority references, financial or service
records, verification results and responsible-owner attestations. Record missing information,
competing explanations and data restrictions. Unresolved authority or rights issues return
to Layer A review; operational uncertainty remains visible in the Layer B record.

## Interpretation safeguard

A variance, unusual payment or weak result warrants analysis; it is not proof of waste, fraud or abuse.

## Accounting distinctions

| Concept | Working definition and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Budget authority / appropriation | Identify the specific authority to incur obligations and its conditions; terminology and legal effect depend on jurisdiction. |
| Obligation | A recognized commitment that may lead to a payment; distinguish new obligations, adjustments and de-obligations. |
| Outlay | In federal spending usage, money paid; do not equate payment with verified delivery or a public outcome. |
| Expenditure | Recognition depends on the applicable accounting framework; record the basis rather than treating all expenditure as cash outlay. |
| Outcome | A change in service quality, access or public conditions, with attribution limits. |

Never add these categories together as though they measure distinct comparable outputs.
See the [federal spending guide and GAO quality review](../Research/sources.md).
Federal award data are not a universal ledger for state/local operations.
