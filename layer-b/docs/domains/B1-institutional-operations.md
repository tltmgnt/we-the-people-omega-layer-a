# B1 — Institutional operations

## Objective

Understand how public institutions translate mandates into coordinated operations while maintaining clear authority and accountability.

These are proposed operational review prompts. Establish applicability with Layer A;
do not infer legal authority or actual institutional performance from checklist completion.

## Research modules

- Organizational mandates and responsibility mapping
- Decision rights, delegation and approval pathways
- Interagency and intergovernmental coordination
- Service ownership and cross-agency handoffs
- Internal controls and operational risk
- Program management and performance review
- Continuity of operations and succession
- Public-facing service standards and complaints
- Corrective action and institutional learning

## Review questions

- Who owns each service stage, handoff and exception?
- Which delegation supports each decision, and where are conflicting approvals escalated?
- How do complaint findings reach an owner who can correct the process?

## Candidate measures

- Unresolved ownership gaps
- Approval-cycle duration
- Service completion and repeated handoffs
- Overdue corrective actions
- Continuity exercise performance
- Share of major processes with accountable owners

Use the [indicator specification](../Methods/scorecard.md): definitions, denominators,
periods, exclusions, uncertainty, owner and revision. Pair speed/cost with quality,
access, safety and rework measures.

## Evidence and escalation

Request relevant authorized process maps, dated authority references, financial or service
records, verification results and responsible-owner attestations. Record missing information,
competing explanations and data restrictions. Unresolved authority or rights issues return
to Layer A review; operational uncertainty remains visible in the Layer B record.

## Interpretation safeguard

Coordination should make responsibility clearer, not allow institutions to shift accountability onto one another.
