# Topological Gap Analysis Ω — Layer B

This is a proposed diagnostic framework, not an externally validated scoring model.
A gap signal is not proof of misconduct or a demonstrated cause.

| Dimension | Operational question | Example gap signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Topology | Are roles, stages and dependencies connected? | Critical task lacks an owner or handoff |
| Power | Are decision rights and approvals clear? | Conflicting approvals stall work |
| Boundaries | Are scope, jurisdiction and responsibilities defined? | Duplication or an uncovered function |
| Information | Are records, finance and status reliable? | Delivery and payments cannot reconcile |
| Incentives | Do targets support the public outcome? | Volume rises while error/rework grows |
| Control | Do authorization, verification and correction work? | Paper controls fail repeatedly |
| Resilience | Can essential operations continue? | One person, system or supplier is indispensable |
| Legitimacy | Are authority, fairness and accountability assessed? | Required authority or review is unresolved |
| Transition | Can changes be introduced safely? | Migration causes service gaps or lost records |
| Institutionalization | Do improvements persist? | Gains disappear after temporary support ends |

## Gap entry

Record gap_id, dimension, severity, affected services, evidence IDs, responsible owner,
dependencies, competing hypotheses, proposed remedy, validation plan and status.
Use LOW / MODERATE / HIGH / CRITICAL as qualitative triage, with written reasons;
there is no validated numerical weighting or misconduct score.

In the draft schema, assessment_basis captures the qualitative severity rationale and
status is OPEN, UNDER_REVIEW or RESOLVED. RESOLVED needs independently checked
remedy evidence in accompanying materials, not merely an administrative closure count.
The validator checks structure and reference membership; it cannot establish a cause,
severity correctness or actual remedy effectiveness.

## Competing explanations

Backlogs can arise from demand, staffing, case complexity, statutory constraints, necessary
safety checks, technology, incomplete inputs or handoffs. Define observations separately
from inferences, identify evidence that would distinguish hypotheses, and test alternatives
before assigning responsibility. Preserve dissent and uncertainty.

A Legitimacy gap routes to qualified Layer A review. High-impact unresolved findings need
a named operational and assurance owner, bounded action proposal and human decision.
