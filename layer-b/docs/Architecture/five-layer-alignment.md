# Alignment with the WE THE PEOPLE Ω initiative

## Goal

Support a practical, nonpartisan civic-systems research, education, evaluation and
institutional-improvement initiative. The five layers and 50-project portfolio are
connected workstreams, not 50 already operating programs or a validated universal standard.

| Layer | Contribution | Layer B interface |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A — Constitutional Legitimacy | Authority, rights, democratic/ethical boundaries, jurisdiction and Tribal sovereignty | Establish applicable boundaries before changes; preserve unresolved holds |
| B — Government Operations | Institutions, funding, workforce, procurement, records, assets and service delivery | Diagnose capacity and execution; propose bounded improvements |
| C — Human and Community Outcomes | Education, health, housing, food, work, safety, mobility, environment, participation and well-being | Pair operational measures with meaningful outcomes and distributional effects |
| D — Intelligence and Evidence | TGA Ω, DTI Ω, HedgeOps Ω, provenance, quality, causal methods, uncertainty, AI governance and independent validation | Make diagnoses and comparisons reproducible; benchmark complex analysis against simpler approaches |
| E — Transformation and Institutional Learning | Pilots, scorecards, review, transitions, leadership, corrections, adoption and learning | Convert supported findings into separately authorized, reversible trials and durable practice |

Operating cycle: establish legitimacy → understand operations → measure human outcomes →
validate evidence → test and implement authorized improvements → verify results →
institutionalize learning. Revisit earlier stages when new evidence, legal questions or
unintended effects arise. The cycle is iterative, not a license to proceed despite a hold.

## Portfolio relationship

See the [source register](../../portfolio/project-register.json) and
[Layer B crosswalk](../../portfolio/README.md).
The register preserves supplied titles and links without claiming that linked contents
were read or that project scope, funding, ownership or implementation was approved.
Formula and curriculum links are recorded separately; no unseen formula is implemented.

Layer D owns shared method development. Layer B documents operational applications of
those proposed methods. Layer E owns transition/adoption and learning design; B does not
treat a favorable diagnostic as permission to deploy. Projects 046–050 are shared support,
not substitutes for institutional authority or independent validation.

## Government collaboration objective

The immediate goal is guidance to an appropriate office for framework review, a preliminary
discussion or one bounded demonstration. Start with a low-risk service process, identifiable
owner and accessible baseline. The [collaboration brief](../Collaboration/concept-and-pilot.md)
defines the proposed conversation and pilot gates without claiming a participating agency.
