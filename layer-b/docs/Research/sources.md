# Official research and data starting points

Checked 2026-10-31. These are discovery/interpretation resources, not a universal legal
rulebook or authorization to obtain protected records. Determine actual jurisdiction,
funding source, current law and institutional authority for each study.

| Source | Use and limitation |
| --- | --- |
| [USAspending Analyst's Guide](https://www.usaspending.gov/federal-spending-guide) | Federal account/award terminology and linkages. Obligations, outlays and loan subsidy cost have different meanings; award data are not the whole government ledger. |
| [GAO Green Book](https://www.gao.gov/greenbook) | Federal internal-control framework; examine applicability before using it for another jurisdiction. The page identifies the 2025 revision as effective beginning fiscal year 2026. |
| [GAO-25-107469 procurement data quality](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-25-107469/index.html) | Documents procurement data-quality concerns. Reconcile timeliness, completeness and accuracy before treating records as verified delivery or outcomes. |
| [California DGS Procurement Division](https://www.dgs.ca.gov/PD/Procurement-Division) | State procurement resources and routes to manuals. Check the current manual, delegated authority, contract type and funding context; do not generalize all state rules to local purchases. |
| [CalHR Workforce Planning](https://www.calhr.ca.gov/about-calhr/divisions-programs/workforce-development-division/workforce-planning/) | California civil-service workforce/succession planning resources; not a universal employee productivity or discipline standard. |
| [NARA records-management FAQ](https://www.archives.gov/records-mgmt/faqs/general.html) | Federal records lifecycle: creation/receipt, maintenance/use and disposition. Identify applicable schedules and access/privacy requirements independently. |
| [OECD Government at a Glance 2025](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/government-at-a-glance-2025_0efd0bcd-en.html) | Comparative public-governance indicators; check definitions, periods, coverage and methods before comparison. It does not validate the proposed Ω methods. |

## Study source register

For each actual source record stable ID, canonical link or authorized file reference,
issuing body, publication/revision/access dates, jurisdiction, authority type, scope,
transformations, exclusions, limitations and the exact proposition supported.

Use a source hierarchy appropriate to the question: applicable law/authority for legal
claims, accounting definitions for financial claims, and documented operational evidence
for service findings. Guidance is not automatically binding law. Public data access is not
a grant of access to underlying protected records.

No live data download, record request, procurement engagement or connection is performed.
